Petzibbo Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026
Developer and data controller: Maryna Verbyk, an independent developer based in Ukraine
Contact: petzibbo.support@gmail.com

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Petzibbo ("Petzibbo," "we," or "the App") handles information when you use our mobile application, create an account, contact support, or save information about your pets. Petzibbo helps you organize pet care and health records. It is not a veterinary service.

2. Information we handle

Depending on which features you use, we handle the following information:

  • Account and contact information: your email address, name, and optional profile details such as a phone number. Authentication is handled by our provider, Supabase. Petzibbo does not store your password in plaintext.
  • Pet profiles: details you enter, such as a pet's name, species, breed, sex, birth date, size or weight, microchip identifier, and photos.
  • Care and health records: food and treat logs, medication and supplement information, stool and symptom entries, vaccinations, procedures, allergies, conditions, health information, reminders, dates, and notes.
  • Files you choose to upload: pet photos, veterinary documents, test results, and other attachments. These files may contain personal or sensitive information that you include.
  • Technical and service information: information needed to operate and protect the service, such as account identifiers, record timestamps, synchronization status, IP addresses or server logs processed by our service providers, and error information. Our legal-information pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process website access metadata under GitHub's own privacy notice.
  • Support messages: the information you provide when contacting us by email.
  • Problem reports sent from the App: if you use Settings → Help & Support → Report a problem, we receive the description you write, the category you choose, and any technical details you type. If you switch on the optional diagnostics setting, we also receive your app version, build number, operating-system version and app language. If you are signed in, the report is associated with your account (we store your account identifier and your account email address is shown to us and used as the reply address). If you are not signed in, you may enter an optional reply email address; it is used only to answer your report. We do not attach your pet records, photos, documents, journal entries, logs or access tokens to a report. Anything you type into a report is sent to us, so please do not include information you do not want us to receive.
  • Abuse-prevention data for reports sent without an account: to limit spam and overuse, a report sent without an account is stored together with a salted, one-way cryptographic hash of the network (IP) address it came from. We do not store the address itself in the report, but the hash is a pseudonymous identifier, not anonymous data: it is derived from your network address, it is stored with the report, and it can be used to recognize repeated submissions from the same address. Reports sent from a signed-in account do not store this hash.

You choose what pet and health information to enter. Please avoid uploading information about other people unless you have permission to do so.

3. How we use information

We use this information to create and secure accounts; save, display, and synchronize pet records across your signed-in devices; provide locally scheduled reminders; generate PDF reports from records you select; respond to support requests and problem reports; limit abuse of the report feature (rate limiting); detect abuse or service failures; and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Where data-protection laws require a legal basis, we generally rely on performance of the service you request, legitimate interests in operating and securing the App, legal obligations, and, where applicable, your consent for optional device permissions or other activities requiring it. You can change camera, photo-library, and notification permissions in your device settings.

4. Where information is stored

With an account: Petzibbo uses Supabase for authentication, cloud database services, and private file storage. Your signed-in pet records and uploaded files are synchronized through these services and cached locally on devices where you sign in. Supabase hosts the account database, authentication records and private uploaded files in the region selected for our Supabase project. Technical operations, support, delivery and service-provider processing may involve other countries. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, we seek to use the safeguards offered by our service providers. Details of Supabase's data practices are available at supabase.com/privacy.

Problem reports: a problem report you send from the App is transmitted to our Supabase project, which processes it and stores it in a database table that the App and other users cannot read. The report is then emailed in plain text by our email delivery provider, Resend, from an address at petzibbo.com that is used only for sending, to our support inbox, a Gmail account (petzibbo.support@gmail.com) operated by Google. The report content, and for signed-in users the account identifier and account email address, therefore also exist in those email copies. Supabase, Resend and Google process this data under their own terms and privacy notices and may operate in other countries.

Without an account (guest/device-only mode): your Petzibbo records are stored locally on your device and are not automatically synchronized to Supabase. If you use Data & Backup to export a backup, that file may include pet records, photos, and documents and is not encrypted by Petzibbo. You decide where to save or share it; protect it carefully. Device or iCloud backups may also be governed by your Apple settings.

Petzibbo currently does not include third-party advertising SDKs or behavioral analytics SDKs. This statement does not exclude the technical logs used by infrastructure providers or data handled by Apple in operating the App Store and TestFlight.

5. When information may be shared

We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. We may disclose information only as needed to: (a) service providers such as Supabase and its subprocessors who operate our infrastructure, Resend (which delivers problem-report emails), and Google (whose Gmail service hosts our support inbox); (b) comply with law or respond to valid legal requests; (c) protect the rights, safety, and security of users or the service; or (d) carry out an action you choose, such as exporting and sharing a veterinary PDF or backup. Files or reports you share outside Petzibbo are thereafter handled by their recipients and the apps you use to share them.

6. Retention and deletion

We generally retain account information and cloud-synchronized pet data while your account is active and as needed to provide the App. You can request account deletion in Settings → Delete account. After successful verification, the account and associated active cloud records are deleted. Removal of associated Storage files may take additional time if a cleanup attempt fails; those remaining private objects are queued for later deletion. Authentication, security and infrastructure logs, and provider-managed backup copies, may persist after account deletion for limited periods defined by those providers' retention practices or legal obligations. A database backup does not necessarily contain the uploaded file objects themselves. We cannot remove copies of data that you have separately exported or shared.

Problem reports

Database copy. Problem-report rows in our Supabase database are scheduled for automatic deletion 90 days after they were created, by a daily job. The job ran for the first time on November 20, 2026 and completed successfully; at that point no stored report had reached 90 days of age, so it had not yet deleted any report. This 90-day schedule applies only to the database rows.

Email copies. The email copy of each report is not deleted by that job. Copies in our Gmail support inbox remain until we delete them, and copies or delivery records held by Resend, Google or other providers are kept under their own retention practices. We do not promise that all copies of a report are deleted after 90 days. To ask us to delete a report you sent, contact petzibbo.support@gmail.com with the details of the report; we will delete the copies we control and, where applicable law requires, ask our providers to do so.

When you delete your account. Deleting your account removes the link between the account and its problem reports in our database (the stored account identifier is cleared). The report content itself, including any reply address, is not deleted at that moment: it remains in the database until the 90-day schedule removes it and in any email copies as described above. Once the link is cleared, we can no longer identify the report from the database by your account, although the text you wrote may itself identify you.

In guest mode, Delete profile removes the device-only pet profile and records managed by the App. Removing the App or deleting an account does not delete copies that you independently exported, shared, or saved to Files, iCloud Drive, or another service.

For questions, access requests, correction, or assistance with deletion, contact petzibbo.support@gmail.com. We respond in accordance with applicable law; we may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

7. Your choices and rights

You can view and edit information through the App, manage optional device permissions, stop using guest mode, or delete your account as described above. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to certain processing, receive a portable copy of information, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You may also have the right to complain to a relevant data-protection authority. Contact us to exercise any applicable rights.

8. Security

We use measures designed to protect account and pet data, including authenticated access, HTTPS connections, and private cloud file storage. No system is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting access to your devices, email account, passwords, exported reports, and manual backup files.

9. Children's privacy

Petzibbo is not designed for children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and take appropriate action. Additional protections may apply under local law.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our features, service providers, or legal obligations change. We will update the effective date and provide additional notice when required. Material changes to how information is used will be addressed before the new practices begin.
